【核心声明】 本应用是一款专为极致数据安全而设计的纯本地单机工具。遵循“零网络通信”与“零知识证明”架构设计。在技术层面，本应用在底层清单中彻底剥离并阻断了所有操作系统网络权限（android.permission.INTERNET 与 android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE），本应用及其开发者绝不收集、不上传、不共享、不出售您的任何个人数据或凭证。

一、 核心原则：零网络通信与物理级沙盒隔离

  1. 彻底禁用网络权限：Android 底层机制物理阻断本应用发起任何网络请求。应用无后端服务器、无远程 API、无云端同步数据库，绝无数据外泄通道。
  2. 本地硬件级端到端加密：所有账号、密码、TOTP 双重验证密钥、助记词及三级备注（备注1、备注2、备注3），均使用 Argon2id 密钥派生函数与 XChaCha20-Poly1305 / AES-256-GCM 高强度算法在设备本地加密后存入私有数据库。
  3. 主密码绝对私有：主加密密钥由您的主密码在本地内存实时派生，受 Android Keystore 硬件安全芯片（TEE/StrongBox）保护，退出锁定后即刻擦除。开发者及任何第三方均无法在外部窥探或解密。

二、 个人信息“零收集”说明

  • 不收集身份信息：不设注册系统，不收集手机号、邮箱、姓名、身份证等个人身份信息；
  • 不收集设备标识：不读取亦不收集 IMEI、MEID、Android ID、OAID、MAC 地址或硬件序列号；
  • 不收集行为统计：不记录使用频率、点击轨迹或页面停留时长，无任何行为追踪探针；
  • 不收集地理位置：不申请、不调用 GPS、基站或 Wi-Fi 定位；
  • 剪贴板保护机制：复制密码仅用于粘贴，支持定时自动清空剪贴板，绝不后台截取外部剪贴板内容。

三、 设备权限申请与合规使用说明

本应用遵循“最小必要原则”向操作系统申请以下权限：

  • 生物识别权限（android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC）：仅用于指纹/面容快速解锁本地数据库。生物识别匹配完全由设备底层硬件芯片完成，本应用不接触亦不收集原始生物特征。
  • 相机权限（android.permission.CAMERA）：仅在您主动扫码添加 2FA 动态令牌时调用，图像流在设备内存本地解码后立即销毁，绝不上传或留存任何画面。
  • 振动权限（android.permission.VIBRATE）：用于操作成功（复制、扫码）时的触觉反馈。
  • 通知权限（android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS）：用于显示剪贴板安全倒计时或自动保存确认提示。
  • 系统自动填充服务（AutofillService）：经您授权并校验指纹后协助填写外部网页或 App 的账密，绝不后台收集其他界面的非凭证文本。

四、 第三方 SDK 与依赖说明

本应用彻底排除了任何第三方商业广告 SDK、友盟/Firebase Analytics 等统计追踪 SDK 以及云端消息推送组件。内置的二维码识别模块（ML Kit / ZXing）完全运行于设备本地 CPU/NPU，离线运行无需联网。

五、 数据自主控制与彻底销毁

  • 完全自主控制：数据完整存放在您设备私有应用沙盒中，您拥有 100% 掌控权。
  • 明文导出警示：导出明文 CSV 脱离了加密沙盒保护，请妥善保管并在使用后安全擦除。导出加密备份（.vgvault）由专属备份密码保护。
  • 彻底物理销毁：在手机设置中点击“清除应用数据”或“卸载应用”，系统将物理擦除所有本地数据库与密钥缓存，设备上不残留任何明文或密文信息。

六、 未成年人隐私保护

本应用不针对未成年人定向推广，亦无账号注册系统。倡导未成年人在法定监护人指导下安全使用密码工具。

七、 政策更新与联系方式

如本政策随技术实践或法律要求变更，更新版本将随应用包及在线公开页面发布。如有任何疑问，请通过开源仓库或应用内“关于”页面提交反馈。

[Core Declaration] VaultGuard is a 100% offline standalone password and 2FA manager designed for absolute privacy. In accordance with zero-network and zero-knowledge principles, all network permissions (android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) have been completely stripped from the application manifest. The application and its developer do not collect, transmit, share, or sell any of your personal data or vault credentials.

1. Core Principles: Zero Network Communication & Sandbox Isolation

  1. Network Permissions Completely Stripped: The Android OS physically prohibits this application from making any network calls. The application possesses zero backend servers, zero remote APIs, and zero cloud databases.
  2. Hardware-Backed Local End-to-End Encryption: All titles, accounts, passwords, 2FA keys, mnemonic phrases, and custom notes (Note 1, Note 2, Note 3) are encrypted on-device before storage using Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305 / AES-256-GCM.
  3. Zero-Knowledge Master Password: Your Master Key is derived in volatile RAM solely from your Master Password, safeguarded by the Android Keystore (TEE/StrongBox), and wiped upon locking. No one in the world can decrypt or inspect your data.

2. Zero Data Collection Statement

  • No Identity Data: No registration required; no collection of names, phone numbers, emails, or IDs;
  • No Device Identifiers: No reading or collection of IMEI, MEID, Android ID, OAID, MAC addresses, or serial numbers;
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: No logging of usage duration, frequency, or click behavior;
  • No Location Data: Zero access to GPS, cellular, or Wi-Fi positioning;
  • Clipboard Security: Copied credentials are placed on the clipboard strictly for manual pasting. An automated clipboard wipe timer is supported, and external clipboard data is never harvested.

3. Operating System Permissions & Lawful Purpose

VaultGuard adheres to the principle of minimal privilege and requests only the following permissions:

  • Biometric (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC): Used solely for fingerprint or facial unlock. Matching is handled directly inside the device hardware security enclave (TEE); the app never accesses raw biometric data.
  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively when scanning 2FA QR codes. Video frames are decoded in volatile memory and immediately discarded.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Provides tactile haptic confirmation upon successful operations.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Displays clipboard wipe timers and autofill confirmations.
  • Autofill Service (AutofillService): Fills credentials into third-party apps and browsers upon biometric confirmation, never capturing non-credential screens.

4. Third-Party SDKs & Components

The application completely strips commercial advertising SDKs, tracking libraries (such as Firebase Analytics, Umeng, Adjust), and cloud push frameworks. On-device QR barcode decoding (ML Kit / ZXing) runs strictly offline on the device CPU/NPU.

5. User Data Ownership & Total Destruction

  • 100% User Ownership: Vault databases reside exclusively within the application's isolated sandbox storage.
  • Backup Caution: Plaintext CSV exports leave the encrypted sandbox; store them securely and shred after use. Encrypted backups (.vgvault) are protected with a dedicated password.
  • Permanent Destruction: Selecting "Clear App Data" or uninstalling the app permanently erases all databases and keys from the hardware storage.

6. Children's Privacy

VaultGuard is an offline utility that collects no data and does not target minors. We encourage parents and guardians to guide minors in safe credential practices.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

Any updates to this policy will be bundled directly into updated application releases and posted publicly. If you have any inquiries, please submit feedback via our repository or the in-app "About" page.
